OUR GOAL

Build a campus where
skills create opportunity.

Our goal is to build a collaborative campus community where Computer Engineering students can turn their technical skills from coding to hardware design into meaningful work and income.

Through freelancing and peer mentoring, we connect students who need project support with students ready to share their expertise. It is a place to earn from what you know, learn from each other, and bring ideas to life.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Curious?
You're in good company.

Here's where to start.

Who is Work 4 A Bit for?

Work 4 A Bit is built for university students who want to find campus services, collaborate on technical projects, share their skills, or learn with a peer.

What's an offer versus a request?

A service offer is an ongoing listing from a provider, such as 3D printing or equipment support. A service request is a one-time need posted by a student looking for help with a specific task.

Can I get help and offer my own services?

Yes. You can find help for one project while offering your own skills on another. Your profile is a place to introduce yourself and the skills you bring.

How is peer mentoring different?

Mentoring focuses on understanding and learning through guidance. Use it to work through concepts, practice a skill, and build confidence while keeping ownership of your learning.

How do we agree on the work?

Discuss requirements, budget, timing, and expected deliverables with the other student before starting. Clear expectations help both of you work together with confidence.
